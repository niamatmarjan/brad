# RelayDesk Product Brief

**Product Name:** RelayDesk — Cross-Team Service Request Platform  
**Target Release:** v0.5.0 (Demo Baseline)  
**Author:** Elena Vance (Product Owner)  
**Status:** APPROVED  

## 1. Executive Summary
In modern cross-functional enterprises, departments (Engineering, Security, DevOps, Data, IT) constantly coordinate critical service requests—such as database provisioning, OAuth credential rotation, data export pipelines, and third-party webhook integrations. RelayDesk provides a lean, transparent coordination platform that tracks requests from initial intake through managerial approval, engineering assignment, peer review, QA verification, and completion with an immutable audit ledger.

## 2. Key Business Objectives
1. **Eliminate Informal Coordination:** Stop service requests from falling through cracks in ad-hoc Slack or email threads.
2. **Explicit Governance Gates:** Ensure no infrastructure or security changes begin without departmental approval.
3. **Rigorous Quality & Defect Retest:** Require explicit QA verification, criteria checks, and zero open defects before any service request is marked complete.
4. **End-to-End Auditability:** Track all state transitions, comments, and decision rationales in an append-only timeline.
