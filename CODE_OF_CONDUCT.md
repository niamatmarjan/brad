# BRAD community code of conduct

## Our commitment

We want developers, researchers, testers, and maintainers to collaborate in a welcoming, respectful, technically rigorous environment, regardless of experience level or background.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss evidence, behavior, and code rather than attacking people.
- Make feedback specific and actionable; acknowledge differences in host/version limitations.
- Credit contributors and sources accurately, including AI-assisted changes.
- Respect consent, privacy, and the boundaries of test environments.
- Help newcomers reproduce issues safely.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, hate speech, or discriminatory abuse.
- Sexualized harassment, unwanted advances, or persistent personal targeting.
- Publishing someone else's personal information, tokens, credentials, or private conversations.
- Deliberately disruptive spam or intimidation, including through review comments.
- Knowingly fabricated benchmarks, test evidence, or claims about another contributor.

## Scope and moderation

These expectations apply to project-controlled discussions, issues, pull requests, events, and official community channels. Maintainers may ask participants to stop, remove comments, limit interaction, temporarily suspend participation, or ban accounts for serious or repeated violations. Decisions should be proportionate and mindful of privacy. Maintainers are also subject to this policy.

## Reporting

Do **not** post a sensitive complaint with private details in a public issue. Use GitHub's private reporting or account abuse-reporting facilities when available, or reach the repository owner through an existing private contact listed on their GitHub profile. A dedicated confidential project mailbox is **not yet published**; if no private route is available, use GitHub's platform abuse-reporting channel. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not the public issue tracker.

## Attribution

This is a project-authored community conduct policy. It is not a claim that the project participates in, or is certified by, any external program.
