# Definition of Ready

A story is ready for implementation when the accountable team agrees that:

- It supports an approved product outcome and has one authoritative native backlog record.
- User, trigger, expected outcome, scope, and explicit non-goals are clear.
- Acceptance criteria are observable and include relevant error/edge cases.
- Business rules, data ownership, and terminology have accountable sources.
- Dependencies and API/schema assumptions are identified; required decisions are accepted or named.
- Risks, security/privacy constraints, and applicable quality attributes are visible.
- A bounded implementation and test approach can be estimated by the delivery team.
- Owner, reviewer, and handoff destination are clear.

BRAD can help route a readiness review to installed native planning workflows; it must not create readiness by inference or write a parallel backlog. This checklist is a recommendation, not a status proven by the Concourse rehearsal.
