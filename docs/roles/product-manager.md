# Product manager / product owner

**Purpose:** Set product direction, prioritize outcomes, make scope and acceptance decisions, and own stakeholder value. BRAD can route a request to an installed native BMAD planning workflow; it does not make product decisions or replace the product owner.

**When to use BRAD:** Turn an approved opportunity into a PRD, refine a specific outcome, decompose approved scope, or inspect delivery evidence. Keep discovery exploratory until the problem and decision owner are clear.

**Example request:** “Use BRAD to help the installed native BMAD product workflow draft a PRD for [initiative]. Preserve these constraints: [list]. Identify assumptions and decisions for me; do not create implementation work until I approve.”

**Workflow and inputs:** Prefer installed BMM Analyst/PM or PRD workflow, then native epic/story planning only after product approval. Provide users, problem evidence, goals, non-goals, constraints, measures, and decision authority. Expected artifacts are a reviewed problem statement, PRD, prioritized outcomes, acceptance criteria, and explicit open decisions. Exact skill names vary by BMAD version and host; inspect the installed skill before invoking.

**Authority and review:** The product owner decides customer value, scope, priority, and acceptance. Human stakeholders approve material scope changes. Review every assumption, success measure, and trade-off; do not treat a generated PRD as customer approval.

**Handoff checklist:** Approved objective and non-goals; named decision owner; measurable acceptance; unresolved questions; priority rationale; linked requirements; next recipient and requested decision.

**Failure recovery:** If evidence or authority is missing, keep the output as a draft and list questions. If BRAD cannot find native BMAD, do not imitate its persona—install only after the project's owner chooses a compatible BMAD version.

**Observed evidence:** Official BMAD v6.12.4/BMM and TEA v1.27.2 were installed in a separate temporary fixture, but no PM or Analyst workflow was invoked by the active host. Concourse includes a fictional sample workspace only; its seeded tasks are not product discovery evidence. This guide is an adoption recommendation, not verified behavior.
